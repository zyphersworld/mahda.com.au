The Waiting List A public, dated record of a DFFH Priority Access housing complaint — and the pattern of other people hitting the same wall.

In August 2026, after a Priority Access application was approved for one bedroom only, a specific two-bedroom property was declined on the stated basis of "extensive maintenance" — no date given. Since then: a conduct complaint to the Victorian Ombudsman, a coordinated escalation to Homes Victoria, and more than a dozen people independently coming forward describing the same one-bedroom-shortage pattern. Warrnambool

Doug Bowen — five years, two children, one wheelchair

Raising his two children alone since his wife's death in 2019 — including his daughter Nova, who has cerebral palsy and uses a wheelchair — Doug Bowen has been on the priority housing list for more than five years. The family has at various points lived in a van, in motels, and couch-surfed. Roma Britnell MP has raised the case in State Parliament and personally visited vacant DFFH properties to verify the department's own vacancy claims. In one report, Mr Bowen said the department never physically met the family in six years to assess their situation.

Source: The Warrnambool Standard, multiple reports 2024–2026
