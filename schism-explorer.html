Schism Explorer

A branching-tree tool for exploring how real religious and mystic traditions have split, historically — each node sourced to actual events and documented causes, not invented for this tool.

What this tool is, and isn't: the tree shape and dates are real history. The optional "Structural Lens" below offers descriptive language from a separate philosophical model — about branching, inheritance, and shared origin in general — as a way of narrating what a tree diagram already shows. It never labels any tradition as more "authentic," "derivative," or "correct" than another, and it doesn't algorithmically sort real denominations into roles. That kind of judgment isn't something a tool should hand down about traditions people actually hold as their faith.
